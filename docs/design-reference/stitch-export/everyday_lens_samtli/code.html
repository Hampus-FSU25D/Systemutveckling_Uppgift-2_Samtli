The Case for Pedestrian-Only Zones in City Centers

Elena Rostova

2 hours ago

I've been reviewing recent studies on urban mobility, and the data seems overwhelmingly in favor of pedestrianizing core downtown areas. Cities that have implemented these zones have seen not only a decrease in localized pollution levels but also an unexpected boost in local retail revenue.

However, the transition phase is often met with significant resistance from local businesses fearing loss of access, and residents concerned about parking displacement.

How can we better design these transition phases to mitigate these concerns while still moving towards a more walkable city model?
